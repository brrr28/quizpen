What Quizpen does

  • A

    From the lesson you taught

    Every question comes from the text you paste, whether it's a textbook page or your own notes.

  • B

    Every right answer explained

    Each right answer has a one-sentence reason that points back to your text. Students see it as soon as they answer.

  • C

    Students play on their phones

    Share a link or a QR code, and students join without accounts. At the end, each one gets a score and a review of every question.
