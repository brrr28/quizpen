What Quizpen does for you

Start with the material you already teach from and finish with questions your students can answer.

  • From your text, in the same language

    Paste a textbook page, your notes or an article. Every question comes from that text. If the text is in Spanish, the quiz is in Spanish too.

  • An answer key you can check at a glance

    Each correct answer has a one-sentence reason from your text. The correct option sits under different letters, A to D, so students can't guess a pattern.

  • Students open it from a link

    Send a link or show a QR code on the screen. Students take the quiz without accounts, and we collect no student data. Prefer paper? Copy the quiz in one click and print it as a worksheet.

Why we're building this

  1. About 1 in 3 teachers in OECD countries already use AI in their work. In Singapore and the UAE it is up to 75%.1

  2. 66% of teachers think AI can give wrong or inappropriate recommendations. That is why every correct answer in Quizpen comes with a reason you can check against your text.2

  3. Quizzing beats re-reading. A week after studying a text, students who practiced recalling it remembered more than students who read it again.3

  1. OECD, Results from TALIS 2024, the Teaching and Learning International Survey.
  2. Education International, "Using artificial intelligence: takeaways from TALIS 2024".
  3. Roediger and Karpicke, "Test-Enhanced Learning", Psychological Science, 2006.

Questions teachers ask

When can I start using Quizpen?

We're opening access in small groups, in the order teachers joined. We'll send one email when your spot opens.

Will it cost anything?

Early access is free for teachers on the waitlist. If we add paid plans later, you'll hear about it before anything changes.

Which subjects and languages does it work for?

Any subject you teach from text: history, biology, geography, literature and more. The quiz comes out in the language of the text you paste. You can see it in the ready examples in Spanish and Polish.

How is this different from ChatGPT or Gemini?

No prompt to write: paste the text and get the quiz. Next to each correct answer is a reason from your text. Students take the quiz from a link and need no accounts. You can use Quizpen alongside them: the quiz copies in one click.

Can I trust the answer key?

AI can still make mistakes, so look the quiz over before class. The reason line makes this quick: it shows which part of your text each answer comes from.

What happens to my text and my students' data?

Your text goes to an AI model only to write the quiz, and we don't store it. Students don't create accounts, and we collect no student data. Paste lesson material and leave out student names and other personal details.
