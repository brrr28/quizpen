What Quizpen does for you

Start with the material you already teach from and finish with questions your students can answer.

  • Works from the text you already teach

    Paste a textbook page, your own notes or an article. Every question comes from that text, so the quiz matches your lesson.

  • An answer key you can check at a glance

    Each correct answer comes with a one-sentence reason that points to what your text says. Its letter, A to D, is picked at random, so students can't guess a pattern.

  • Ready for the board or for paper

    Run the quiz on screen as a warm-up, or print it as a worksheet for the last ten minutes of class.

Why we're building this

  1. About 4 hours a week is how long a typical lower secondary teacher spends marking and correcting student work.1

  2. 5.9 hours a week is the time teachers who use AI tools at least weekly say they save.2

  3. Quizzing beats re-reading. A week after studying a text, students who practiced recalling it remembered more than students who read it again.3

  1. OECD, TALIS 2018 Results, OECD average for lower secondary teachers, as summarised by ACER.
  2. Gallup and Walton Family Foundation, 2025. Survey of 2,232 US public school teachers; time saved is self-reported.
  3. Roediger and Karpicke, "Test-Enhanced Learning", Psychological Science, 2006.

Questions teachers ask

When can I start using Quizpen?

We're opening access in small groups, in the order teachers joined. We'll send one email when your spot opens.

Will it cost anything?

Early access is free for teachers on the waitlist. If we add paid plans later, you'll hear about it before anything changes.

Which subjects and languages does it work for?

Any subject you teach from text: history, biology, geography, literature and more. The demo writes the quiz in the language of this page, Ukrainian or English, even if your text is in the other one.

What happens to the text I paste?

It goes to an AI model only to write your quiz, and we don't store it. Paste lesson material and leave out student names and other personal details.
