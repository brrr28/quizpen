What you get from one text

Use the text you already teach from. Quizpen turns it into five questions for your class.

  1. 1

    Questions only from your text

    Quizpen asks only about what is in the text you paste, so the quiz covers what you taught. A text in Spanish gives you a quiz in Spanish.

  2. 2

    A key you can check quickly

    Each right answer has a one-sentence reason that points to your text. Right answers fall on different letters from A to D, and wrong options are about as long as the right one. Students can't pick the answer by its letter or its length.

  3. 3

    On phones or on paper

    Students open the quiz from a link or a QR code on the board. For a paper version, copy the quiz in one click or print it.

Questions teachers ask

When can I start using Quizpen?

We open access in small groups, in the order teachers joined. You'll get one email when it's your turn. Ready examples work right now.

Will it cost anything?

Early access is free for teachers on the waitlist. If we add paid plans later, we'll tell you before anything changes.

Which subjects and languages does it work for?

Any subject you teach from text, such as history, science, geography or literature. The quiz comes out in the language of the text you paste. Open the Spanish and Polish examples in the demo to see it.

How is this different from ChatGPT or Gemini?

In a chatbot you write the prompt and set the format yourself. In Quizpen you paste the text and get five questions with a reason next to each right answer, ready for students to play from a link. The quiz copies in one click, so you can keep using the tools you have.

Can I trust the answer key?

AI can still make mistakes, so look the quiz over before class. Each reason shows which part of your text the answer comes from, so you can check every answer against the text.

What happens to my text and my students' data?

Your text goes to an AI model only to write the quiz, and we don't store it. Students don't create accounts, and we collect no data about them. Paste lesson material only, without student names or other personal details.
