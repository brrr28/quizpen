§ 1.2

Exercises

  1. Exercise 1.1

    From your text only

    Each question and its right answer come from the text you paste. The quiz covers what your class read.

  2. Exercise 1.2

    A reason next to every right answer

    Next to each right answer, one sentence points to the part of your text that supports it.2

    Example 1.2

    From a Year 7 science page, “How plants make food”

    Why do most leaves look green?

    1. AThey absorb green light
    2. BChlorophyll reflects green light (right answer)
    3. CTheir cells hold green water
    4. DSunlight is mostly green

    2The page says chlorophyll reflects green light, so green is the color that reaches our eyes.

  3. Exercise 1.3

    Ready for class by link, QR or print

    Students open the quiz on their phones from a link or a QR code. You can also copy it or print it.3

    3Students play without accounts, and Quizpen collects no data about them.
